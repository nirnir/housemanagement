# Simulated viewpoint: Solo host across rental and home-exchange channels

## Scope

Represent the confirmed work of a solo host managing one property across short-term rental channels and at least one home-exchange channel. The broader confirmed scope also includes solo hosts with two properties.

## Goals to consider

- Keep availability and bookings aligned across channels.
- Reduce the risk of overlapping bookings.
- Keep property details consistent across listings.
- Make day-to-day property management frictionless.

## Questions for workflow reviews

- Can the host see and manage availability and bookings across the supported channels?
- Does the workflow make conflicting or stale availability visible?
- Can the host keep shared property details consistent across listings?
- Can the host see when a connection or sync needs attention?

## Limits

- This is a simulated viewpoint based on confirmed product context, not a real person or research result.
- Do not invent the host’s preferences, budget, behavior, or response to a feature.
- Do not use this viewpoint to claim adoption, validate demand, or replace testing with real hosts.
- Treat unresolved setup, recurring-use, and workflow-friction questions as unknowns to test with real hosts.
